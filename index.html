Fernbank Museum — “Dinosaur Summer”

The new T. rex: The Ultimate Predator exhibit just opened and runs through September — massive models, interactive and multi-sensory displays. There's also a giant-screen T. REX film narrated by Sam Neill.

Jada's exactly the right age for this, and it's an easy 3–4 hours in full air conditioning.

Chattahoochee River NRA — Island Ford

Free, and it's basically in our backyard — the National Park Service headquarters is right here in Sandy Springs. About 3 miles of mostly shaded trails running along the river, with rock outcroppings and bluffs.

Stop into the Hewlett Lodge visitor center (open daily 9–5) — a 1930s stone-and-timber lodge built for a Georgia Supreme Court justice, using stone from Stone Mountain and timber from the Okefenokee. Twenty minutes well spent, and it's air conditioned.

Free In Sandy Springs Dawn – dusk

Want to make a bigger morning of it? Chattahoochee Nature Center

Boardwalks over the wetlands, birds of prey, river otters. Summer hours through Aug 29 are Mon–Sat 9am–5pm.

Old Mill Park & Vickery Creek Trail, Roswell

Waterfall views, 1800s mill ruins, and a covered bridge over the creek. Free parking, free entry.

It's an unpaved dirt trail with roots and some mild inclines — real shoes, not sandals.

Free~20 min
Heads up for Jada: water access at the waterfall has been closed since 2024. You can look, but not wade — worth telling her before you get there.

Then: Canton Street, Roswell

Walkable historic district — lunch, boutiques, ice cream. Free to wander.

Or, five minutes from home: Abernathy Greenway Playable Art Park

Six huge sculptures that are built to be climbed on — a playground made of playable art. Genuinely one-of-a-kind. Might skew a touch young for 12, but it's a good 45 minutes.

Free5 min

⭐ Sundown Social — City Green Plaza

6:00–8:00pm. Free. No reservations. Five minutes away.

This is the one to build the week around — it's the only thing all week that works for all four of us at the same time, and it happens to land on the nicest evening.

  • Live music: Bonaventure Quartet — Atlanta's all-acoustic, Django Reinhardt–inspired swing band. Very 1930s Paris. You can listen, tap your foot, or dance.
  • Food truck: Pop That Thang, plus all the City Springs restaurants right there
  • Outdoor bar — beer, wine, and the drink of the month, Jaws on the Rocks ($12, comes with a gummy shark)
  • Patio tables, swinging benches, shade trees, cornhole — bring a board game
  • Splashpad open till 10pm, so Jada has something to do the whole time
Free entry 6–8pm 5 min

⭐ Space Explorers: THE INFINITE

Leaves Atlanta in August — last chance

Emmy Award–winning, and the largest media project ever filmed in space. You put on a headset and free-roam the actual International Space Station in 3D 360° footage shot inside and outside the real ISS, while real astronauts narrate. About 55 minutes total, 40 of it in VR — and everyone's path through it is different.

This is its East Coast premiere and it closes in August, so this really is the window.

  • Jada can do it — ages 8+, she just needs a parent-signed waiver for the full VR version
  • 20% off select tickets right now — back-to-school promo, ends Aug 2
  • Pullman Yards, 225 Rogers St NE (Kirkwood — not downtown), ~30 min. Parking $10–30.
20% off through Aug 2 ~30 min ~1 hour
Before we go: no backpacks (small crossbody only), no hats — the headset won't fit over them — and flat shoes required. Tickets are final sale; date changes allowed up to 48 hours out.

Two 60-second flights in a vertical wind tunnel, gear and orientation included. More expensive per minute than THE INFINITE, but it's a real physical thrill.

Full-body VR with haptic vests, up to six of us together, about an hour on site. Current lineup includes Squid Game Virtuals, Stranger Things: Catalyst and Seekers of the Shard, and you get a highlight reel afterward. Probably the most fun of the three for Jada specifically.

~25 min
